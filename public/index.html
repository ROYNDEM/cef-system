About the Forum

The County Energy Forum is a high-level intergovernmental and multi-stakeholder convening bringing together senior government leadership, Members of Parliament, county governments, development partners, and the private sector.

Registration is strictly by invitation and prior confirmation. All participants will receive a unique QR code upon approval for fast, secure check-in on the day of the event.
