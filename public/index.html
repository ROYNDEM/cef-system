County Energy Forum

📅 25 – 27 May 2026  |  Nairobi, Kenya

About the Forum

The County Energy Forum is a high-level intergovernmental and multi-stakeholder convening bringing together participants, including senior government leadership, Members of Parliament, county governments, development partners, and the private sector.

The Forum provides a platform for high-level dialogue and decision-making on energy policy, county energy programmes, and strategic partnerships. It is designed to ensure a seamless, secure, and protocol-compliant experience for all participants.

Registration is strictly by invitation and prior confirmation. All participants will receive a unique QR code upon registration for fast, secure check-in on the day of the event.
